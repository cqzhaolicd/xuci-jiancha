11. 居家办公 Work from Home —— 重点句式

——《我等不及要向他们展示我们的新房子了！》
—I can't wait to show them our new house!
——爸爸，今天下午您能顺路载我去商店吗？
—Dad, can you give me a ride to the store this afternoon?
—Sorry, I have to work from home all day.
——你能扫一下地吗？
—Can you sweep the floor?
—OK, Mom. I'll sweep it right away.
——午饭后，我能和朋友们出去玩吗？
—Can I go out and play with my friends after lunch?
—Sorry, Jim. You have to put away your things first.
——我可以把一些花放在桌子上吗？
—Can I put some flowers on the table?
—Sure. / Of course.
