  • 推理：推断作者观点、态度、写作目的

🧱 词汇积累（一）compare / perform / solve

  • compare v. 比较 → compare...with...／compare...to...／compared with
  • perform v. 表演；表现 → perform a play／performer 表演者／performance 表演
  • solve v. 解决 → solve the problem／the solution to the problem

🧱 词汇积累（二）serious / similar / accident

  • serious adj. 严肃的；严重的 → be serious about／take sb. seriously
  • similar adj. 相似的 → be similar to／similarity n.（反义 be different from）
  • accident n. 事故 → by accident 偶然地／accidental adj.／accidentally adv.

🧱 词汇积累（三）expect / as a result of / touch

  • expect v. 预料；期待 → expect to do／expect sb. to do／be expected to do／expectation n.
  • as a result of 由于（后接名词/短语）｜as a result 结果
  • be filled with = be full of 充满
  • touch v. 触摸；触动 → touch one's heart／keep in touch with／touched 感动的／touching 令人感动的

🧱 词汇积累（四）space / please / sense / though

  • space 空间；太空 → in space／in one's spare time
  • please v. 使满意 → pleased 高兴的（be pleased with）／pleasant 令人愉快的／pleasure 愉快
  • sense 感觉；意义 → a sense of humor/responsibility／make sense 讲得通／common sense 常识
  • though 虽然；不过 → as though 好像／even though 即使

📐 语法精讲：最高级的变化规则

  • 单音节／部分双音节：① 一般 +est（small→smallest）② 以 e 结尾 +st（large→largest）
  • ③ 辅音字母 + y 结尾，变 y 为 i +est（happy→happiest）
  • ④ 重读闭音节双写末尾辅音字母 +est（big→biggest）
  • 多音节／部分双音节：most + 原级（important→most important）

📐 不规则变化 + 五大用法

  • 不规则：good/well→better→best｜bad/badly/ill→worse→worst｜many/much→more→most｜little→less→least｜far→farther/further→farthest/furthest
  • ① one of the + 最高级 + 名词复数：one of the biggest animals
  • ② the + 序数词 + 最高级 + 名词：the most important traditional festival
  • ③ 有物主代词/指示代词/名词所有格时不加 the：my best friend
  • ④ 副词最高级前 the 可省略：He runs (the) fastest. ⑤ 常与 in/of/among 连用

📐 最高级常见句型 5 条

  • A + be + the + 最高级 + in/of/among…（Alice is the tallest in her class.）
  • one of + the + 最高级 + 名词复数（Beijing is one of the biggest cities in China.）
  • What/Who + be + the + 最高级 + in/of…？
  • 最高级 + 现在完成时：He is the best student I have ever met.
  • 最高级前有序数词/物主代词/指示代词/名词所有格修饰时不加 the

🎯 四类练习 + 学法建议（若琳）

  • 春节选词填空：tradition→traditional／celebrate→celebration／lucky／wealthy
  • 克服恐惧短文填空：fear→fearful／confidence↔confident／breath↔breathe／relaxed
  • 如何交朋友补全短文：friendly／friendship／patience／truly／safety
  • 学法：① 先把讲义空白处自己填一遍；② 对照答案红笔订正；③ 把最高级三条例外和词形变换写进错题库，隔天复做
