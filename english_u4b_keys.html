四、重点句式 Key Sentence

火车行程长达五个多小时，之后我们换乘了一辆公共汽车，于下午抵达了我外公外婆所在的村庄。
The train journey lasted more than five hours. After that, we took a bus and arrived at the village where my grandparents lived in the afternoon.
另一种（更简洁的）表达：
After a train journey of over five hours, we took a bus and got to my grandparents' village in the afternoon.
记忆提示：① familiar 考点是搭配 be familiar with（人熟悉物）/ be familiar to（物为人所知）； ② matter 常考 It doesn't matter.（没关系）与 What's the matter?（怎么了）； ③ describe → description 是典型 v.→n. 变化，写作常用； ④ nod 是重读闭音节结尾，过去式/现在分词都要双写 d。
