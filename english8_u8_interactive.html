Unit 8 · How do you make a banana milk shake?

人教版八年级上册 · Unit 8 · 8 题 · 10 卡牌

祈使句

以动词原形开头，表命令/建议：Cut up the bananas. Turn on the blender.

祈使句否定

Don't + 动词原形：Don't pour the milk into the blender.

How many

问可数名词数量，后接复数：How many bananas do we need?

How much

问不可数名词数量：How much milk do we need?

量词表达

a cup of 一杯、a teaspoon of 一茶匙、a piece of 一片、a glass of 一杯（玻璃杯）。

量词复数

量词变复数，名词不变：two cups of yogurt、three pieces of bread。

顺序词

first 首先、then 然后、next 接下来、finally 最后，用于描述制作步骤：First, peel the bananas. Then, cut them up. Finally, put them into the blender.

制作词汇

peel 削皮、cut up 切碎、pour 倒入、mix 搅拌、add 加入、turn on 打开。
