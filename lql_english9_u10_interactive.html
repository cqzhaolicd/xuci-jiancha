Unit 10 · You're supposed to shake hands.

人教九全 · Unit 10 · 16 题 · 10 知识 · 10 卡 · 6 错

单元话题 · 风俗与得体行为 (customs)

本单元围绕各国 customs(风俗习惯)与不同场合“该做什么、不该做什么”展开：见面礼仪(第一次见面怎么问候)、拜访礼仪、时间观念、餐桌礼仪。Language Goal: Talk about customs and what you are supposed to do. 核心句型：be supposed to do / be expected to do(应该、按理应做)+ It's impolite/important to do sth(做某事是不礼貌的/重要的)。单元话题句：You're supposed to shake hands.(你应该握手。)

be supposed to do = should do · 应该做某事

结构：主语 + be(am/is/are；过去 was/were)+ supposed to + 动词原形。肯定表“应该做”：You're supposed to arrive on time.(你应该准时到达。) You're supposed to shake hands.(你应该握手。) 否定在 be 后加 not，表“不应该做”= shouldn't do：You're not supposed to smoke here.(你不应该在这里吸烟。) 疑问句把 be 提到句首：Am I supposed to wear jeans?(我应该穿牛仔裤吗?) 过去时表“本应该做(常含没做到)”：I was supposed to arrive at 7:00, but I arrived at 8:00.

同义表达 · be expected to do

be expected to do sth 意为“按习俗/常理应当、被期望做某事”，与 be supposed to do 意思基本相同，可互换。例句(1c 对话)：A: What are people in Korea supposed to do when they meet for the first time? B: They're supposed to bow. 而 In the United States, they're expected to shake hands. Grammar Focus：—Am I supposed to wear jeans? —No, you're expected to wear a suit and tie. 两者后都接动词原形；expected 更强调“大家普遍预期如此”。比较：You're supposed to be there at noon. = You're expected to be there at noon.

见面礼仪 · 各国第一次见面怎么做 (1a/1b/2d)

第一次见面各国习惯不同：日本——鞠躬(初次见面通常 bow；1b 中日本人 Yoshi: We bow.)；韩国——同样鞠躬(And in Korea we also bow.)；巴西——有时亲吻脸颊(kiss)；美国、墨西哥及多数西方国家——握手(shake hands；1b 中墨西哥人 Rodrigo: In Mexico, we shake hands.)；法国——见面时互相亲吻脸颊两侧(2d: French people are supposed to kiss when they see each other.)。课文 2d：约翰向日本男孩 Sato 伸出手，Sato 却鞠了躬——That's how people in Japan are expected to greet each other. It's impolite if you don't bow.(在日本，见面不鞠躬是不礼貌的。)

准时文化 · 瑞士 vs 哥伦比亚 (3a 对比阅读)

瑞士学生 Marc(洛桑)：In Switzerland, it's very important to be on time.(在瑞士守时非常重要。) We're the capital of clocks and watches, after all!(毕竟我们是钟表之都!) 约好 noon 见面就 expected to be there at noon；迟到哪怕 15 分钟，朋友就可能 get mad；所以他 make an effort to be on time(努力守时)，认为 it's impolite to keep others waiting；而且从不未打电话就登门(never visit a friend's house without calling first)，见朋友几乎总先 make plans。哥伦比亚学生 Teresa(卡利)：We're pretty relaxed about time.(我们对时间相当放松。) 不喜欢 rush around(匆匆忙忙)，赴约稍晚一点没关系(don't mind if people are a little late)，珍惜与家人朋友共处的时光(value the time)，有空常直接 drop by 朋友家，不必提前约。→ 3a 答案：在哥伦比亚，晚餐晚到 15 分钟是可以接受的。

餐桌礼仪小测验 · 世界各地 (Section B 1a)

课本 1a 判断正误：①印度——应该用手抓饭吃(In India, you're supposed to eat with your hands.)，T；②中国——不该把筷子插进食物里(not supposed to stick your chopsticks into the food)，T；③韩国——最年幼的人被期待先动筷(the youngest person is expected to start eating first)，F(应是最年长者先吃)；④法国——面包应放在桌上(not on the plate)，T；⑤中国——用筷子敲空碗不礼貌(it's impolite to use your chopsticks to hit an empty bowl)，T。

中国的餐桌礼仪 · Steve 的中国之行 (Section B 1b/1c)

Steve 将去中国留学，朋友 Yang Ming 告诉他中国的餐桌礼仪：不要把筷子插进食物/米饭里(You're not supposed to stick your chopsticks into your food.)；用筷子指着任何人是不礼貌的(It's impolite to point at anyone with your chopsticks.)；同桌有老人在场时不应先动筷(You shouldn't start eating first if there are older people at the table.)。归纳：插筷、用筷指人、敲空碗是中式餐桌“三忌”。

Section B 2b · 林月 (Lin Yue) 在法国的餐桌挑战

阅读是 Lin Yue 写给 Laura 的信：她在法国的交换生活很愉快，寄宿家庭 go out of their way to make her feel at home(费尽心思让她有宾至如归之感)，祖母还特地学做中餐；她最大的挑战是餐桌礼仪(learning how to behave at the dinner table)：面包不能放盘子里，要放在桌上(not supposed to put your bread on your plate — supposed to put it on the table!)；除了面包，不能用手指拿任何食物吃，连水果也要切碎用叉子吃；说自己吃饱了是不礼貌的(It's impolite to say you're full.)，不想再吃就说 That was delicious.；手肘不能放桌上(not supposed to put your elbows on the table)。她 gradually getting used to it(逐渐习惯)，不再觉得法国风俗奇怪。

句型 It's + 形容词 + to do sth

句型：It's + impolite/polite/important/necessary + (for sb) + to do sth，意为“做某事是(不)礼貌的/(不)重要的”。it 是形式主语，真正的主语是后面的不定式 to do。Grammar Focus 例句：Is it impolite to keep others waiting? Yes, it's very impolite to keep others waiting.(让别人等着是非常不礼貌的。) Is it important to be on time? Yes, it's important to be on time.(守时很重要。) 课本 3a 还有 it's polite for boys to shake hands and for girls to kiss each other on the side of the face；也可用 It's impolite if you don't bow. 的 if 从句形式。

重点词汇与短语速记

词汇：custom 风俗；bow 鞠躬；kiss 亲吻；greet 问候；relaxed 放松的(be relaxed about time 对时间不紧张)；value 重视/价值；drop by 顺便拜访；noon 正午；mad 生气的(get mad 生气)；effort 努力(make an effort to do 努力做)；passport 护照；chalk 粉笔(clean the chalk off the blackboard 擦掉黑板上的粉笔字)；northern 北部的(northern coast 北部海岸)；season 季节；knock 敲(门)(knock before entering 进门先敲门)；eastern 东部的；worth 值得的(It is worth the trouble. 值得费这番工夫)；manner 方式/礼貌(table manners 餐桌礼仪；mind your manners 注意礼貌)。短语：be supposed to 应该；shake hands 握手；drop by 顺便拜访；after all 毕竟；get mad 生气；make an effort 努力；stick...into 把……插入；go out of one's way (to do) 特意/费尽心思(做)；get used to (doing) 习惯(做)；gradually 逐渐地。
