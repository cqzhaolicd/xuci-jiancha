Unit 3 · I'm more outgoing than my sister.

人教版八年级上册 · Unit 3 · 8 题 · 10 卡牌

单音节比较级

形容词 + er：tall→taller、short→shorter、small→smaller。

多音节比较级

more + 形容词原级：more outgoing、more beautiful、more careful。

特殊变化

good/well→better，bad→worse，many/much→more，little→less。

比较级句型

A + be + 比较级 + than + B：Tom is taller than Jim.

as...as 原级比较

as + 原级 + as 表示'和……一样'，中间必须用形容词/副词原级；否定用 not as/so...as：He is as tall as his brother. / This movie isn't as interesting as that one.

比较级修饰语

a little、much、even、far、a lot 可放在比较级前加强程度：She is much taller than me. / This book is even more interesting than that one.

越来越…

比较级 + and + 比较级：warmer and warmer、more and more beautiful。

拼写规则

重读闭音节双写尾字母：big→bigger；辅音+y 变 i：heavy→heavier。
