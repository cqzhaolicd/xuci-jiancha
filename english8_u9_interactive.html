Unit 9 · Can you come to my party?

人教版八年级上册 · Unit 9 · 8 题 · 10 卡牌

邀请句型

Can you come to my party? 你能来参加我的聚会吗？

接受邀请

Sure, I'd love to. / Of course. / Yes, I can.

拒绝邀请

Sorry, I can't. I have to... / I'm afraid I can't. 并说明原因。

have to

不得不、必须做，三单用 has to：She has to do her homework.

must 与 might

must 必须（mustn't 禁止）；might 可能（表不确定）：You must finish your homework. / I might be late.

星期表达

Monday 周一、Tuesday 周二、Wednesday 周三、Thursday 周四、Friday 周五、Saturday 周六、Sunday 周日。

现在进行时表将来

I'm visiting my grandparents tomorrow. 表示已安排好的计划。

礼貌用语

I'm afraid... 恐怕……；Thanks for asking. 谢谢邀请。
