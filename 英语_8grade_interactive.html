英语 · 8年级课堂笔记

人教新目标上册 · 10 单元 · 16 题 · 12 卡牌

Unit 1 Where did you go on vacation?

一般过去时（不规则动词）；复合不定代词 something/anything/nothing；Where did you go? 假期话题。

Unit 2 How often do you exercise?

频度副词 always/usually/often/sometimes/never；How often 提问频率；every day/week。

Unit 3 I'm more outgoing than my sister.

比较级：-er/more + adj；than 比较；as...as 同级比较；两者比较。

Unit 4 What's the best movie theater?

最高级：-est/most + adj；the best/the most；评价比较（comfortable seats, cheapest）。

Unit 5 Do you want to watch a game show?

动词不定式 want to do；谈论电视节目（sitcom/talk show/game show/news）；love/enjoy+doing。

Unit 6 I'm going to study computer science.

be going to 表将来计划；职业词汇（scientist/engineer/pilot）；How are you going to do that?

Unit 7 Will people have robots?

will + 动词原形表将来；there will be；一般将来时疑问句；预测未来（robots/free time）。

Unit 8 How do you make a banana milk shake?

祈使句 First/Then/Next/Finally；量词 a cup of/two spoons of；食物制作步骤。

Unit 9 Can you come to my party?

情态动词 can 表邀请；接受/拒绝邀请的表达（Sure, I'd love to. / Sorry, I have to...）；时间表达。

Unit 10 If you go to the party, you'll have a great time!

if 条件句（主将从现）；should 提建议；谈论后果与建议。
